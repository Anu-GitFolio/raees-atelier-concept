# Asset provenance

## Product photography

The local WebP files named after catalogue handles reference images returned by the public Raees Al Oud catalogue on 16 September 2026. Each product in `server/catalog.json` has its original product-page URL. Images were resized and compressed; product depictions were preserved.

These assets remain the property of their respective owners. They are used as reference material in this independent job-application concept. Commercial reuse requires permission. There is no claim of endorsement or ownership.

## Editorial image

`oud-hero.webp` and `oud-hero-small.webp` show an unbranded editorial still life used to introduce the collection. It shows unbranded agarwood and an amber oil bottle and does not represent an actual catalogue item. No source-store product or certification claims are attached to it.

## Typography and icons

Manrope, Cormorant Garamond, and Noto Sans Arabic are self-hosted from the installed Fontsource packages. Their SIL Open Font License notices are included under `public/assets/licenses`. No remote font service receives customer requests. Simple interface icons and the text favicon are inline SVG geometry.
