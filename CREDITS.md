# Credits

## Product data and photography

Product names, prices and photographs come from the public [Raees Al Oud catalogue](https://raeesaloud.com), checked on 16 September 2026. Each entry in `server/catalog.json` includes its product-page URL. Local photographs were resized and compressed to WebP.

Product photographs remain the property of their respective owners. Commercial reuse requires permission. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Raees Al Oud.

## Editorial artwork

`oud-hero.webp` and `oud-hero-small.webp` depict an unbranded still life of agarwood and an amber oil bottle. The image illustrates the collection's theme; it does not depict a catalogue product.

## Fonts and icons

Manrope, Cormorant Garamond and Noto Sans Arabic are self-hosted from Fontsource packages. Their SIL Open Font License notices are included in `public/assets/licenses/`.

Interface icons and the text favicon use inline SVG.
